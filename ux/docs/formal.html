Lean and RhoApp#

Generated apps keep a shallow Lean 4.12 model and vendor the shared RhoApp library. The model is deliberately shallow: it does not formalise Rholang syntax, ERTP, phlo or cryptography, and a proved-model result is not a refinement of deployed Rholang.

RhoApp#

formal/lean/RhoApp is extracted from r-share’s proven Basic.lean and Linear/{Cell,Protocol,Deploy,Concurrency}.lean (AGPL-3.0-or-later, commit 7114acba). It provides:

  • the linear cell store (peek, take, put, call, commit), WF, and the D1/D2/D3 rejection theorems;
  • helper protocols H1, H2a, H2b and P2;
  • deploy atomicity: deploy_atomic_rollback, run_failure_no_leak, commit_preserves_wf;
  • concurrency: commit_commute, run_disjoint_commute, disjoint_cells_linearizable;
  • shared quantities: Amount, Height, Bps, floorDiv, ceilDiv, share, sumNat.

Its axiom audit reports only propext and Quot.sound.

Vendoring#

A generated app contains:

spec/lean/RhoApp.lean
spec/lean/RhoApp/Basic.lean
spec/lean/RhoApp/Linear/{Cell,Protocol,Deploy,Concurrency}.lean
spec/lean/RhoApp/Audit.lean
spec/lean/VENDORED.json

VENDORED.json records the source, version, source commit (or working-tree) and a deterministic SHA-256 over the vendored paths and bytes. The app’s make formal-check runs lake build, tools/check_vendored.py and tools/check_obligations.py; a tampered file or a forbidden Lean escape fails.

rhobuilder --json formal check --project /path/to/app
rhobuilder --json formal check --project /path/to/app --strict
rhobuilder --json formal sync  --project /path/to/app --source /path/to/rhobuilder

check_obligations.py reads spec/flows/core.yaml. Every component with mutating arms must declare lean_status; owed is accepted by default and rejected by --strict; a non-owed status must name a theorem. sorry, admit, axiom and native_decide are forbidden. This checks the model obligation, not a refinement of deployed Rholang.

Status vocabulary#

proved-model means proved about the shallow model. proved-conformance additionally needs a theorem, a corpus vector and a suite assertion. owed is first-class. Never write verified or a claim of Rholang refinement.

The bookable-service example carries a shallow dispute model: voteOnce proves the second vote precondition fails, provider exclusion is explicit, and settleConserves proves settled + refund = amount. Its spec/flows/core.yaml names voteOnce as proved-model, so formal check --strict passes for that example. Generic scaffolds keep their arms owed.

The app keeps its own Basic, Invariants, Emit, Audit and Model/*; Emit and Audit are app-specific names and vectors and are not moved into RhoApp.
