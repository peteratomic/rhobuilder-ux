Evidence#

The machine-readable P5 records are:

  • evidence/p5-devnet.json — the five pinned roots, the hand-authored bookable-service root, and nine suite results.
  • evidence/p5-generic-ledger.json — the generic payments-ledger root, its four generated contracts, and the static/smoke checks.

They contain no keys. The studio renders both records, including suite statuses, with a raw-JSON disclosure. evidence/testnet-campaign.template.json is a template, not a record; the UI excludes .template.json files from the measured-evidence feed.

Reading it#

  • baseline names each app, its pinned commit, root URI, status and step count.
  • app names the generated app root and status.
  • suites.results is the same pass/fail/not_run record written to deployments/suite-results.json.
  • notes records the r-cash core-profile decision and the zero-duplicate adopt run.
  • The generic ledger record also lists .rhobuilder-contracts.json and notes that its design arms remain owed; it has no behavioural suite.

Evidence is not authority. The live check is baseline verify --deep, which re-resolves every root at a finalized block. See Testing and gates for status meaning and Glossary for not_run/owed/verified.

What is deliberately absent#

  • No public-testnet execution record, because no live campaign has been approved.
  • No key material.
  • No claim of Rholang refinement; per-app Lean obligations remain owed.
